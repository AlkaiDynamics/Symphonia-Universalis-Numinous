## Reference-object rule

The fixed reference object is **music-native structure itself**, not any interpreted SUN model. The versions in this archive are formal accounts, protocols, or candidate generalizations around that musical evidence. External bridges/adapters are distinct from both the music and the models.

## Provenance rule

`SOURCE SAYS X` ≠ `WE INFER X` ≠ `HYPERGRAPH DERIVES X`.

Unretrieved historical material is marked as such rather than reconstructed from later versions.
